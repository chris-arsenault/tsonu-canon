# Field Encyclopedia expansion — 2026-09-20

Completed and subsequently committed in `6dfe576`. Counts and verification
below describe this batch; its original no-publication note is historical.

## Scope

Added **24 Encyclopedia entries**: 10 lifeforms, seven technologies and seven resources. Each prevalence has eight entries. Extended eight existing Encyclopedia articles and 14 existing Atlas articles. Added one Atlas faction, **the Ilvaren**, and no NPCs.

All 32 new or extended Encyclopedia articles name Atlas locations. All 24 new types have references from Atlas prose; 23 also name a person, faction, vessel or artifact in their own articles. The adoption supplies particular specimens, cultivation, equipment, trade and household use. It does not require a campaign to follow a shared plot.

For a short review, start with [Oravel](../world/geographic_location/oravel.rb), [Iridess](../world/geographic_location/iridess.rb), [the Ilvaren](../world/faction/ilvaren.rb) and [Quiet Measure](../world/ships/named/quiet_measure.rb).
