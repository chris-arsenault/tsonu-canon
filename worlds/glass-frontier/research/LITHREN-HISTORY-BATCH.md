# Lithren: discovery, settlement and inherited history

Final Lithren batch: eight new Atlas entries, fourteen expanded entries and twenty dated moments. The modern history runs from 2272 to 2435, with documented landfall in 2311 and continuous settlement from 2322. Ancient history remains a set of evidenced local sequences.

## The modern history

| Date | What changed |
|---|---|
| 2272–2286 | [Elian Soreth](../world/npc/elian_soreth.rb), a Pelhari astronomer, predicts an outer planet and organizes observations that locate it. His work remains local during the Signal Famine. |
| 2307–2311 | [Vedra](../world/npc/vedra.rb) turns back from an unsafe approach, then makes the [documented landfall](../world/incident/lithren_landfall.rb). Damaged pre-Glassfall charts leave earlier knowledge uncertain. |
| 2318–2322 | Intermittent camps become a lasting foothold when Vedra grounds [Sevrin](../world/transport/sevrin.rb) and converts it into occupied rooms. Families and service trades gradually accumulate. |
| 2346–2352 | [Sereyat](../world/artifact/sereyat.rb), an ancient musical instrument, is sold and performed on Pelhari. Collector voyages finance housing and further expeditions. |
| 2361–2368 | Disappointing finds and costly voyages shrink the trade. Departing families sell rooms cheaply; owners of freight and stores acquire more power. |
| 2372 | The [Sevrin Stand](../world/incident/sevrin_stand.rb) defeats an armed eviction. Households keep their homes, but property and wealth remain unequal. |
| 2383–2401 | [Ressa's grandmother](../world/npc/ressa_dorr.rb) builds the eating hall. Independent provisioning and specimen commissions sustain livelihoods. She buys Sereyat back for local performances. M5a consolidated her biography into these surviving owners. |
| 2408–2435 | Tavia's comparative work enters an existing community. The established returns, Witnessings and expeditions follow. Ressa inherits Leth's business in 2426; its rooms, customers and debts still matter. |

[Ithara](../world/geographic_location/ithara.rb) owns the settlement's growth and contraction. [Lithren](../world/cosmology/lithren.rb) owns the wider discovery account. The [full chronology](STORY-FOUNDATIONS.md#ancient-sequence-and-modern-dates) connects these additions to the previous batches.

## Ancient lives carried by present places

- **Sereyat:** an ancient player used a lowered bank, and a later repair retained that accommodation. Ressa now weighs keeping the instrument against selling or touring it to finance her garden.
- **Oravel:** a broad heat circuit was partly abandoned while smaller rooms received new fittings and continued use. The Hanging preserves the alteration. This establishes local change without explaining the planet's climate.
- **Damarat:** floor layers show changed production and work taking space formerly used for rest and meals.
- **Ithara's courts:** a planting bed became a path while an adjoining entrance remained in use. Its cause and date relative to the fighting remain open.
- **[Ivara](../world/artifact/ivara.rb):** Osen's trail reaches a partly unloaded carriage at Damarat. Matching pieces and a witnessed unloading establish one destination after Sahel's Breach. The carriage can be repaired for modern use; the travelers' later fate remains unknown.

## Scope and review

The additions explain present lives and give existing places older layers. They add no geographic hub, faction, mantle or fundamental law. The ancient enemy, ecological causes, final fate and possible origin of resonance remain intentionally open. The gate Return remains unperformed.

The episode research was *Enterprise*, “Terra Nova,” through the [official synopsis preserved by TrekCore](https://ent.trekcore.com/episodes/season1/1x06/synopsis.html). Family memory and surviving household objects supplied color; its lost-colony revelation and rescue plot were not adopted.

Canon checks pass for both active worlds; all 93 Glass Frontier starting locations retain their focus coverage. The local reader build succeeds. The GM-note check still reports one unchanged issue in `room_before_the_ice`; this batch adds none. Detailed evidence is in [the working plan](STORY-RANGE-PLAN.md#final-batch-execution).

No gameplay trial establishes whether agents now choose a wider range of stories. Ithara now has the graph's highest reported connection count, which is a reason to stop expanding it and inspect discovery balance separately. Lithren content expansion stops here.
