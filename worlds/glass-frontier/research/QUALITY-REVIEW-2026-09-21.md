# Glass Frontier quality review — 2026-09-21

Reviewed checkout: `3653dc6`. The findings below record that checkout. The revision account at the end describes subsequent local changes; the findings are retained as evidence of what prompted them.

## Assessment

The recent expansion contains distinctive physical material. Its recurring weakness is the presentation of that material: careful demonstrations, explanations of what a discovery cannot establish, and repeated assemblies of familiar interested parties. Older connected articles contain more overt narrator commentary and definitions by negation.

The most useful revision would preserve the discoveries, histories, ordinary pleasures and mysteries while making their accounts less defensive and their local relationships more distinct. A wholesale reduction of entries would discard much of the strongest work.

## Coverage and method

Four review agents examined separate areas, with the primary reviewer reading the sixteen excursions and checking findings against source bodies. Coverage overlaps; the following figures must not be added into a unique-entry total.

- Latest Encyclopedia batch: all 33 new articles and all six expanded existing articles, plus relevant Atlas adoptions and neighbors.
- Lithren: 49 canonical bodies, including the principal places, modern history, artifacts, creatures, expedition, supporting people and external neighbors.
- Istrava: 45 canonical bodies, including mantle history, principal places, factions, ecology, cultural material and external comparisons.
- Excursions: all 54 Atlas bodies forming the sixteen sets. Additional reads included Prismwell, Blue Step Works, Fermata, Nara Pell, Renn Duvasi, Life in the System and Roads of Refuge. An initial combined display of Blue Step Works was truncated; findings below do not rely on its unread tail.
- Older Bloom/Shear and shared services: 24 complete bodies, including the principal places and institutions, several creatures and materials, Keel, Vantara, Outside and Cold Lantern.

Source bodies, relevant neighborhood queries and the current `web` report supplied evidence. Search matches and graph degree were discovery aids, not proof of weak writing. This is a substantial targeted review, not a complete review of the world's 1,035 entries. No entries received human-review declarations.

All source links below point into the canonical `world/` directory. Line numbers refer to the reviewed checkout.

## First corrections

### 1. A summary settles a future that its source leaves disputed

**High priority; factual framing.** [Istravan War](../world/thread/istravan_war.rb), line 4, describes “the tyranny foretold after liberation.” [Talessar](../world/artifact/talessar.rb), lines 28–30, instead preserves several readings: Aren's own rise, a successor's tyranny, or a preventable danger. Its history includes a warning whose pictured deaths were prevented.

Change the summary to describe opposition divided over a feared second tyranny or the disputed crown image. A structural summary can steer subsequent authors even though it is not a public article. Preserve the artifact's evidence and open outcome.

### 2. Important people have lost their names while retaining their biographies

**High priority; specificity and continuity.** [Araket Seizure](../world/conflict/araket_seizure.rb), lines 12–23, gives an orc physician a practice, medical boundaries, patients, a son and correspondence. She recurs in Anaret and Ithara. Her established name, **Sira Dhen**, survives in the editorial log at line 8.

[Ithara Comparative Expedition](../world/faction/ithara_comparative_expedition.rb), line 30, and the [survey](../world/incident/ithara_survey.rb) and [tool return](../world/incident/ithara_tool_return.rb), line 11 in each, repeatedly describe “the founder.” Her historical acts and relationships survived consolidation, but **Tavia Mereth** survives only in the expedition's editorial log.

The same problem occurs without a recoverable name at [Charethis Household](../world/faction/charethis_household.rb), lines 10–16: “the owner” has distinct tastes, gives orders and controls the retreat, yet remains a generic villain-shaped vacancy.

Restore the two established names and identify Charethis's central owner. Choose an appropriate discoverable representation without recreating an oversized NPC roster. Incidental buyers, workers and children can remain unnamed; these examples concern recurring particular people whose identity already carries substantial lore.

### 3. Public prose repeats the author's safeguards

**High priority; pervasive framing.** Examples include:

| [Ithara](../world/geographic_location/ithara.rb):93 | “Neither site's visitors need a commission from the gate expedition.” |
| [Damarat](../world/geographic_location/damarat.rb):26 | “neither requires returning the whole building” |
| [Anaret](../world/geographic_location/anaret.rb):49 | “without exhausting the reasons to make them” |
| [Ivara](../world/artifact/ivara.rb):26 | “No great Return is needed.” |
| [Istrava](../world/geographic_location/istrava.rb):27 | “Coremark does not administer its towns or supply every force here.” |
| Istrava:29 | “the war does not place every world under attack” |
| Istrava:39 | “Their value does not grant the party holding them the right to rule…” |
| [Issava Tending](../world/encyclopedia/ability/issava_tending.rb):15 | “leaving the unusual recognitions reported aboard Olenvar as separate observations” |
| [Suralier](../world/encyclopedia/role/suralier.rb):15 | “rather than to everyone who operates a crystal window” |

These statements often defend sound decisions: independent exploration, a regional war, ordinary repair, genuine uncertainty, and a profession with its own identity. Their surrounding facts frequently establish those decisions already.

Replace the reassurance with the positive fact where one is missing; remove only redundant commentary. Name actual local governments and suppliers, show Tamet making the part, describe the gardener releasing a plant that still turns toward its chosen visitor. Attribute a claim about rightful rule to people who hold it. Preserve measured limits, actual prohibitions and deliberately unknowable ancient history.

### 4. The new Encyclopedia batch follows one conspicuous shape

**High priority; cumulative effect.** All **33 new articles have exactly one prose block and exactly three prose paragraphs**. Twenty-four files have exactly 22 lines. A separate read-only count confirmed the agent's measurement.

Typical progression: explain the mechanism, name an adoption, finish with a limitation or another use. [Ilath](../world/encyclopedia/culture/ilath.rb), [Uthrel](../world/encyclopedia/lifeform/uthrel.rb), [Suralier](../world/encyclopedia/role/suralier.rb) and [Ulren, now Esken](../world/encyclopedia/technology/esken.rb) exemplify the shape despite describing very different subjects.

The associated Atlas additions often become miniature lessons: an unnamed learner or maker demonstrates the subject, encounters its limitation, then compares or practices. [Selk](../world/installation/selk.rb):15, [House Delven](../world/installation/house_delven.rb):16 and [Caldris](../world/installation/caldris.rb):17 are individually useful examples of the recurring treatment.

Revise according to the subject's missing substance. Ilath can carry beliefs and internal disagreement; a profession can have a recognizable artistic vocabulary; a small device can remain short. Do not manufacture different paragraph counts merely to hide the pattern. Preserve the existing observations and consequences.

## Relationships and adventure texture

### 5. Too many Istravan destinations offer the same faction gathering

**Medium-high priority; overconnection.** [Theskar](../world/installation/theskar.rb):19 gathers resistance, Severance and Deral Hunters at tables where “an arrival can choose whom to approach.” [Kethra](../world/installation/kethra.rb):19 gathers Severance, Detha, Serevans and hunters. [Veyr](../world/installation/veyr.rb):34 again brings Severance, Serevans and Detha together. [Tovanesh](../world/installation/tovanesh.rb):37–39 assembles resistance, Veyr performers, Tereval patrons and Severance.

These are plausible connections, several supported by ordinary work and family ties. The problem is that traveling between them repeatedly exposes much the same social world. The descriptions begin resembling selectable contacts arranged around different scenery.

Give each place a characteristic intersection and distinguish standing presence from a particular visit. Theskar's mechanics, detained families and armed escort already supply substantial action. Keep Tereval's collecting, performance and occupation patronage; a mantle intervention can be a disruptive particular event. Do not remove links merely to lower degree.

### 6. Lithren repeatedly funnels discoveries into the same purchase dispute

**Medium priority; repeated stakes.** [Ilven Sarith](../world/npc/ilven_sarith.rb):19–21 seeks the Damarat Bed and an uninterrupted Oravel sheet. [Eveli](../world/artifact/eveli.rb):17 adds his purchase offer for a machine its customers need. [Kavren](../world/creature/kavren.rb):24 adds his demand for an intact living fold. [Vannor Account](../world/rumor/vannor_account.rb):18 gives him first purchase of movable assemblies. [Damarat](../world/geographic_location/damarat.rb):37 adds another buyer wanting a whole fitted door.

Ressa, Tovin and Osen also repeatedly supply, carry and guide these discoveries. A hub benefits from familiar people, and Ilven's acquisitiveness is coherent characterization. But the varied finds repeatedly ask the same question: accept payment and remove the thing, or preserve local access.

Retain the developed Damarat dispute. Give selected other finds pressures arising from their particular behavior, uses or histories. Replacing Ilven with a second collector everywhere would reproduce the problem.

### 7. Different enemy forces repeat the same balanced moral composition

**Medium priority; formula.** [Velisar](../world/geographic_location/velisar.rb):19, [Vardesh](../world/transport/vardesh.rb):13 and [Naloven](../world/geographic_location/naloven.rb):36 each distinguish willing supporters, compelled personnel and people remaining for family or personal obligations.

Those distinctions matter, especially given Aren's power. Their recurrence makes successive enemy forces feel assembled from the same explanatory ingredients. Let the general account own the broad composition; particular units need different dominant experiences and behaviors. Preserve morally complicated individuals where they change a specific encounter.

### 8. Several mysteries converge on comparative observation

**Medium priority; cumulative sameness, not a demand to solve mysteries.** The gardeners compare sibling plants; Eshrel passengers compare room sketches; Heskar visitors compare flashes and drawings; Delven's wrights copy signals; Ildara pilgrims compare depicted places; Selk attendants compare sensations with measurements. The relevant bodies are [Olenvar Gardeners](../world/faction/olenvar_gardeners.rb), [Eshrel Companions](../world/faction/eshrel_companions.rb), [Heskar Dome](../world/installation/heskar_dome.rb), [House Delven](../world/installation/house_delven.rb), [Ildara](../world/installation/ildara.rb) and [Selk](../world/installation/selk.rb).

Each investigation has tangible evidence, which is a strength. Several also offer physical action already: move Heskar's telescope, enter Vell's passage, dive after a shedder, board Uldevan. The repetition lies in where the narration spends its attention and where subsidiary accounts end.

Deepen those different activities and let selected local investigations produce bounded, public discoveries. A reachable room, recovered person or identified object can settle a particular question while the larger phenomenon remains open. Do not impose answers on Lithren's ancient antagonist, ecological history, fate or origin of resonance. Do not turn every small mystery into permanently inconclusive scholarship either.

### 9. Aldevra's physical adventure is more specific than its reward

**Medium priority; generic treasure.** [Aldevra Relief](../world/artifact/aldevra_relief.rb):14 promises “worked precious metal, wearable ornaments and an elaborate weapon.” [Aldevra](../world/installation/aldevra.rb):15 describes “worked treasures” in counterweighted cradles. The sunlit approaches, submerged stair and changing loads are vivid; the objects attracting everyone remain interchangeable treasure.

Identify one desirable piece by its physical function, appearance and local significance. Make its removal or use matter through the existing environment. This need not create another artifact article unless that particular object warrants one. Leave the remaining treasury open to later discoveries.

### 10. Some good cross-pollination is told repeatedly

**Medium priority; duplication.** [Tovanesh](../world/installation/tovanesh.rb):22–24 already describes shipboard pupils, cramped turns, recordings returning home and local teachers learning from them. The new passage at :27 repeats the exchange through a teacher losing a return bout. [Tovan Yield](../world/encyclopedia/ability/tovan_yield.rb) describes the same route again.

Integrate the named technique into the existing account. Keep a separate passage only for a different consequence. The exchange itself is strong shared-civilization material.

Similarly, several Drail and Vell Encyclopedia articles independently send performers to Fermata. Those connections are justified, but most say only that performers visit and learn. Deepen one exchange through an actual difference between the places instead of repeating the itinerary.

## Naming and older material

### 11. Ulen and Ulren are too easily confused

**Medium priority; naming.** [Ulen](../world/encyclopedia/technology/ulen.rb) is a recovered balancing mechanism; [Ulren, now Esken](../world/encyclopedia/technology/esken.rb) is a rescue enclosure. Both belong to Lithren expedition vocabulary, and Ulen Walking adds another nearby use. The one-letter distinction carries no established relationship.

Give the newer rescue enclosure a more audibly distinct name, updating its canonical uses and lexicon together. Its pressure petals and treatment gloves are worth preserving.

### 12. A veiled creature's type does not explain its defining difference

**Medium priority; unsupported classification.** [Lithren Frost Page-Eater](../world/creatures/veiled/lithren_frost_page_eater.rb):4–9 eats ink and reproduces the text in frost, while declaring `type_of :lacunae`. [Lacunae](../world/encyclopedia/lifeform/lacunae.rb):39–45 remove inscriptions from stone and avoid Print-covered surfaces.

An exceptional individual could reconcile these accounts. The present hook does not establish that relationship. Decide whether the animal is an observed exception or a different creature, and make the observable distinction legible without supplying a secret cause. Neither veiled status nor brevity alone is a defect.

Lacunae also merit a separate emphasis correction: their plates, long-lived home fields and digging are vivid, but cues and variations dwell on checking pairs, countersigning and making loss “schedulable” (:49–53). Preserve copying as one consequence while giving the animal's encounters more room.

### 13. Fermata contains the clearest overt slop in the inspected shared hubs

**High priority within a later legacy rewrite.** [Fermata Station](../world/locations/settlements/fermata_station.rb) contains:

- :70: “which is its own kind of commentary.”
- :89: a word for failing the Sit-In is mentioned but never supplied, followed by “which somehow makes it worse.”
- :96: “nothing is illegal, exactly” and “the consequences are real and everyone knows what they are,” without those consequences.
- :108: the narrator diagnoses some dissidents as “broken people” and declares others “not entirely wrong.”
- :113: “This is not metaphor.”
- :119: an uncertain temporal effect followed by “which is itself a political act.”
- :122–126: abstract thematic oppositions instead of events, interests or actions.

The Drone's load-bearing music, restricted political standing and physical consequences of a Session are substantial. Rewrite around those facts and the existing named participants. Supply the particular consequence where the narrator currently gestures toward it. The unexplained “Footprints” and “year 138” at :94 also need an in-world referent and chronology check.

The [Life in the System](../world/pages/life_in_the_system.rb) overview has a milder version: “None of this is exotic,” “This is not advanced technology,” and “These are not exotic.” Its useful information is ordinary supply, affordable resonance equipment and tourism. State those facts directly and use a few existing examples where broad claims about sports and leisure remain generic. This does not call for making routine life dangerous.

### 14. Vantara's sweeping claims erase the surrounding civilization

**High priority; inconsistent world framing.** [Vantara](../world/npcs/factions/vantara.rb):59 says it maintains route infrastructure because “nobody else was going to do it.” At :66 the narrator endorses the claim that before Vantara there were no rest facilities, rescue coverage or reliable schedules. Its own :55 acknowledges competing operators, while [Keel](../world/locations/landmarks/keel.rb):73–75 describes section-by-section services maintained by carriers, ports, guilds, cooperatives and public services, with named examples.

The historical claim needs evidence, and the present account obscures actual alternatives. State Vantara's service footprint and distinguish it from others'. An expansive company claim can be attributed to the company. Preserve standardized commercial comfort as a useful part of ordinary civilization; remove the narrator's verdicts about “soulless” branding, “bland competence” and the equally valid sides of an argument.

### 15. One connection asserts the opposite of the supplied evidence

**High priority; semantic graph error.** [Empty Bearing](../world/creatures/anomalies/empty_bearing.rb):25 declares that the predator `depends_on` [Third Window](../world/artifacts/field/third_window.rb). Its explanation describes crews using a protective hood and decoys against the predator. Third Window's own account describes a fitted hood damaged on a retreat.

Nothing in those bodies establishes the animal's dependence on this particular hood. Preserve the actual encounter and its prose reference. Replace the edge only if a truthful typed relationship describes that event; do not choose a convenient verb to maintain degree. This is a concrete example of a connected graph whose connection does not mean what the lore says.

The fourth reviewer also flagged [Cold Lantern](../world/locations/waypoints/cold_lantern.rb):101–103: an edge to the Empty Bearing is justified by general hushmaw precautions. Review whether that named individual has actually affected the waypoint before retaining a particular-animal relationship.

### 16. Stillloom omits the material transformation its operation requires

**High priority; missing physical account.** [Stillwater](../world/encyclopedia/resource/stillwater.rb):42–46 defines purified Echo River water with a suspended crystal storage lattice. [Stillloom](../world/encyclopedia/technology/stillloom.rb):10–11 and :36 describes weaving “live stillwater culture” into damping cloth. The loom's account supplies an elven culture frame but no organism, carrier material or conversion from the defined liquid medium into living threads.

Read together, the articles leave a material relationship unexplained beneath confident terminology. Decide whether the loom uses a separate living culture or incorporates crystal-bearing water into a textile substrate, and let the appropriate entry own the physical account. Keep the origin of the recovered frame uncertain if appropriate. The excellent consequence—a passing tram leaving a flaw in the cloth—should survive the correction.

### 17. Deep Shear promises wonders but withholds the examples

**Medium-high priority; generic science-fiction description.** [Deep Shear](../world/locations/landmarks/deep_shear.rb):65–67 says substances have “only recently begun to acquire names” without supplying one, lists valuable “tissue, shells, fluids, and shed parts,” then says “The biology does not always remain biology.” Its closing at :85 is “Somewhere in the inner Deep, things that nobody has charted continue to be there.”

Use a few actual named materials or organisms and one observed change after recovery. Finish with a particular current condition or observation. The pocket geography, hush, delayed instruments and changing travel distances are already strong; preserve those and the open extent of the Deep.

### 18. Coremark's political protection remains a gesture toward a scandal

**Medium priority; refusal to locate consequential action.** [Coremark](../world/npcs/factions/coremark.rb):65 describes settlements protecting smugglers and civic leaders conducting unrecorded business, including Accord members, without locating an example. It ends, “The conversation, if it ever happens, will be uncomfortable.”

Name one actual port arrangement and its visible consequences. Maintain the secrecy of other routes where that is justified. Describe the syndicate's good internal treatment and violence against outsiders without the narrator's preemptive “not malicious” judgment at :42.

### 19. Older institutions share the same weary narrator

**Medium priority; emotional and rhetorical cloning.** [Shear Compact](../world/npcs/factions/shear_compact.rb):43–55 describes universal complaints, universally preferred mediation and the forecast “It won't work forever.” [Tempered Accord](../world/npcs/factions/tempered_accord.rb):48–50 calls mediation universally slow and preferable, then standards resented by everyone outside Sithari and exploited by everyone inside it. [Bloom Coalition](../world/npcs/factions/bloom_coalition.rb):91 judges its present-day invocations “mostly empty.”

These are different institutions rendered through the same generalized cynicism. Attribute opinions and describe actual outcomes. Their administrative functions belong in the setting; the correction is not to remove mediation, standards or coordinated rescue.

### 20. Some older statements undermine chronology, geography and campaign variety

**Mixed priority; correct facts first.** [Bloom Coalition](../world/npcs/factions/bloom_coalition.rb):60 calls 2380–2396 an “eighteen-year run,” borrowing the longer emergency interval that began in 2378. Other passages repeat the conflation through correctly computed spans with the wrong starting event. Distinguish the emergency response from the institution and use its actual dates.

[The Shear](../world/locations/regions/the_shear.rb):53 describes interstitial debris, explicitly excluding a band or shell, while its relationship prose at :108 calls it a “turbulent band.” Align the relationship with the actual geography. [Ironwhistle](../world/creatures/anomalies/ironwhistle.rb):39 gives a Compact identification in 2338, before the Compact's 2383 foundation. Retrospective classification is possible; state that if it is intended rather than silently changing a date.

[Bloom Zones](../world/locations/landmarks/bloom_zones.rb):78 tells the table, “Any orbit the party is standing in can become a containment site.” New zones and the Xyloathax precedent are legitimate canon. The broad invitation nevertheless encourages the same containment interruption in unrelated settings. Give its scene-entry note an evidenced condition that reflects the event's exceptional scale. Preserve Bloom's established system-wide danger without promoting it as the default explanation for every adventure.

## Revision approach

1. Correct the prophecy summary, false dependency, historical/geographic mismatches and missing material account; restore missing identities and resolve the unsupported creature/type relationship.
2. Remove repeated editorial reassurance and narrator verdicts while preserving facts and uncertainty.
3. Differentiate faction presence, recurring discovery pressures and particular rewards.
4. Reshape the Encyclopedia accounts around their subjects: beliefs and disagreements for traditions, sustained practices for occupations, observed mechanisms and variation for organisms and materials. Integrate duplicate adoption passages.
5. Develop a few of the grounded exchanges above, then read neighboring articles consecutively to check whether they still sound like the same lesson.

Review each affected article and its neighbors as a whole. Rewrite sections, move passages and reorganize complete accounts wherever their structure reproduces the weakness. Preserve the established ideas and their strongest concrete details. Article length and the number of changed files do not determine whether the correction is sufficient.

## Revision account

The local revision develops the approved subjects without restarting their generation. Frontier accounts now distinguish what people are doing at each place. Istrava's visitors, occupants and troops have different immediate activities; completed visits no longer assert a standing faction presence. Lithren's objects lead into flawed manufacture, blocked growth, thermal exposure and competing futures as well as collecting. Sira Dhen and Tavia Mereth retain their biographies under their names. Mareva and Ilessa Tereval, and Ordan Vesh, are named within existing household accounts.

Culture and occupation articles have been reorganized around beliefs, exchanges, inherited objects, bodily care and sustained practice. Concrete animal accounts such as Emri and Othri retain their existing substance. The rescue enclosure is now Esken, separating its name from Ulen frames. Stillwater, Stillloom and Stillhull share a stated material mechanism: deliberately written damping signals in crystal-bearing liquid, sealed inside fibers and hull cells.

Excursion revisions give investigation some definite results: a Selk journey locates a blocked culvert, a Heskar replay resolves a mistaken exchange, and separated Uldevan guests find one another at a recognizable table. The broader causes and unexplored spaces remain open. Aldevra's pictured prize has a physical form and surviving fragment; Charethis has a named collector with habits that shape a raid and escape. Other passages now concern a returned plant, a racing crew's altered vessel, ribbon dancers and a displaced predator rather than repeating a lesson followed by a comparison.

Six exchanges connect existing subjects through their consequences: avreth cultivation and Veyr performance; Delven's optics and Istravan suraliers; flooded and dry balancing platforms; a Sereyat copy adapted for an injured Naloven musician; tovan yielding against Arev's continued motion; and a passive Esken at Cold Lantern. Vell's borrowed-object costumes also travel to Fermata on visible cords. The avreth musician remains on Lithren preparing to depart. The Shear hunter/velith proposal was left undeveloped; the existing music exchanges already supply that connection without another added traveler.

Older articles received substantial revisions, especially Fermata, Vantara, the damping materials, Lacunae and the Compact. Coalition chronology distinguishes the 2378 emergency from its 2380–2396 institutional life. Ironwhistle's old encounter receives retrospective identification, and the Shear remains interstitial debris. False dependency edges were removed. Shared civilization retains independent carriers, municipal facilities and differing local authorities.

Independent rereads covered the rewritten frontier accounts, the changed Encyclopedia accounts and their adopted uses, the revised excursions, and the older material and place articles. They caught and corrected accidental pronoun changes, an unsafe rescue transfer, duplicated incident ownership, a missing public account of distant responses to Istrava, and several smaller continuity errors. No human-review declarations were added. The guidance now directs authors to reconsider whole articles and neighboring accounts when the same buyer, lesson or caution repeats.

### Final scope and verification

Three further independent rereads checked the revised frontier, Encyclopedia, excursion and institutional accounts. Their remaining corrections distinguish the enuli touching a pilgrim from the distant animal supplying perception, leave the avreth performance's specific arrangements with Veyr, and align Coremark's identity card with its visible offices and senior route officers. Tamet has declined the offer for Eveli and accepted a commission for contemporary panels; his ambition to spread and improve manufacture gives that account a different future from Damarat's removal dispute. The House Tereval log now accurately describes name discovery through prose.

The changes touch 69 Atlas entries, 20 Encyclopedia entries, the Istravan War thread and Life in the System. Ten Atlas files received relationship-text clarification only; the remainder include revisions of differing scale. The Esken rename replaces Ulren and adds no net Encyclopedia entry. Guidance, the naming declaration and this report accompany the lore changes.
