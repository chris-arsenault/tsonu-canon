# Lithren: first batch for review

Twenty new entries connect Ithara's residents, two expedition destinations and the gate undertaking. This brief covers the substantive additions; the links are optional deeper reads. The batch contains seventeen Atlas entries and three Encyclopedia articles, integrated with seven existing entities.

## Places and journeys

**[Damarat](../world/geographic_location/damarat.rb)** is an occupied workshop terrace above unexcavated machinery. Its ancient forming bed makes replacement equipment from supplied stock. Workers sleep between finished panels awaiting collection. Below their rooms are counterweight shafts, a trapped passage and racks of settings for shapes whose original use remains unknown.

**[The Damarat Removal](../world/conflict/damarat_removal.rb)** begins with an attractive offer: sell the machine, clear debts and leave Lithren. Some residents want exactly that. Others have built homes and livelihoods around it. Removing the bed requires opening the occupied floor, and the buyer's guards are already surveying the freight approach. Exploring the lower works, protecting a load, helping a family leave and finding a way to keep local manufacture viable lead in different directions.

**[Oravel](../world/geographic_location/oravel.rb)** is a deep hall divided by silver and violet metal sheets. Suspended galleries move on balancing frames; taking away a valuable fitting can change the balance of the route used to carry it home. There are upper landings, a repaired crossing and tight service passages behind the sheets. The lowest floor remains unreached. Recoverable cargo lies beyond a span that can carry people but cannot support the abandoned load.

The **[Oravel Crossing](../world/incident/oravel_crossing.rb)** gives these journeys a shared recent history. Daret supplied rigging; Tovin supplied shelter and gave up his cargo to bring injured workers home. One worked for someone he had robbed. The rescue produced lasting cooperation while the earlier theft remained unpaid.

**[Ithara Gate](../world/installation/ithara_gate.rb)** now has physical workings: curved barrier tracks, counterweights, inspection passages and an adjoining workshop court. The detached **[Ninth Leaf](../world/artifact/ninth_leaf.rb)** demonstrates a useful defense with a second release behind the apparent guard position. Daret wants access from both occupied sides; Selven wants his people controlling the release chambers. The composite return remains unperformed.

## People to meet again

- **Ressa Dorr:** feeds crews and advances provisions, profits from wealthy patrons upstairs, and is saving for a real garden. She serves Tovin meals while recovering the cost of his thefts.
- **Tamet:** an orc maker whose repairable contemporary machine, Eveli, carries knowledge out of the excavations. He wants credit for his own work and affordable equipment for his neighbors.
- **Ilven Sarith:** a wealthy collector who appreciates workmanship, pays for skill and exploits a seller's need for immediate money. His purchases can fund a departure or remove something a camp depends upon.
- **Tovin:** a carrier captain and armed cargo thief. He has rescued people without charging them and fired at people who resisted a robbery. His ship, Ulveth, puts passengers beside tools, cargo and one another's bunks.
- **Kel'esh:** a gnomish materials investigator pursuing a particular unexplained ancient join, even when the next observation may earn nothing. They fund some journeys through practical testing work.
- **Daret Valis:** Nereth's younger sister and an Iral rigger. She values Selven's pay and rescue commitments, works well with his opponents and has told her crew she would refuse an order to close a barrier on rival excavators.

Their common meeting place is [Ithara](../world/geographic_location/ithara.rb), especially Ressa's tables and Tamet's adjoining bay. Provision runs, private purchases, science and shared equipment connect them without making everyone part of one expedition.

## Discoveries that can recur elsewhere

- **Varal:** adjustable ceramic-rod forming beds. Ancient and contemporary examples differ in precision, size and repairability.
- **Ulen:** nested balancing frames. Their weight passages can provide routes through buildings; altered loads and broken restraints make familiar mechanisms dangerous.
- **Istril:** layered metal that spreads heat along a sheet, with recognizable faces, difficult folds and valuable offcuts. Its manufacture remains only partly understood.

These articles establish particular capabilities and variations. They do not enumerate the kinds of work that may exist elsewhere on Lithren. The ancient enemy, ecological causes, ultimate fate and origin of resonance remain open for every audience.

## Episode influence and checks

The researched episode was *Deep Space Nine*, **“Explorers”**. Its [visible craftsmanship and shared working space](https://www.startrek.com/en-un/news/lower-decks-inspires-look-back-ds9-explorers) informed Eveli's hand controls, Ulveth's cramped accommodations and affection expressed through repair and meals. The episode's voyage and discovery plot were not adopted.

World validation and lint pass with zero findings. All three new locations meet the focus requirement; the graph retains its existing disconnected cases. The GM-note audit reports no new-entry issue and one existing repetition finding in Room Before the Ice. Public pages and the local reader data build were checked. No gameplay trial or hosted deployment was performed.

This completes the first batch. M4 remains open for fuller development of the recoverable district; M5's longer departure trail remains separate.
