## What the assessment measures

The desired result is a place where an unfamiliar detail can be learned, used, valued or lived with. Entry totals describe the work; they do not prove that result. The following evidence was used to judge the batch, without turning each question into a requirement on each article.

| Question | Evidence in the written material |
|---|---|
| Can observation change someone's understanding? | An emri nest reveals nearby material but can contain modern contamination. Othri guarding a carried handle distinguish an occupied nursery from loose salvage. Irul's colors depend on illumination. A sural image can move while the object stays still. |
| Can someone acquire useful knowledge or skill? | Six new techniques cover aerial movement, plant handling, escape from a hold, sash fighting, a mountain launch and balanced crossings. Their applications differ with partners, loads, terrain and opponents. Existing Echoing, Unclosing and Poise have concrete uses alongside them. |
| Is there something worth wanting besides the central answer? | Living avreth for instruments, a velori harvest, a healthy second generation of specimens, a fine thavren color, a familiar face screen and an intact urom impression have value even if a mystery remains open. |
| Do people have lives around these things? | Unfolders breed their own stock; households hold second suppers; children gather fruit and keep mineral-building animals; performers choose instruments and images; hosts fit borrowed bodies; pilgrims exchange drawings. These activities have participants without adding named NPCs. |
| Does knowledge travel without making places identical? | Ulen walking changes between Oravel's galleries and Aldevra's flooding rooms. Orveks encounter hot grit, wet cliffs and carved ornament. Tovan turns differ between a pool and a ship. Varashi has household and estate forms. Vell's costume practice travels without exporting its guests as a dependable phenomenon. |
| Is the material actually present in the world? | Every new article has named Atlas references and an authored use in Atlas prose. The local passages identify an occupied case, a practice, a crop, a purchase or another concrete circumstance. Two racing vessels also declare the new Caldris skiff type. |

The flora and fauna are especially different in how people encounter them: breeding, harvesting, gardening, collecting, watching, avoiding and protecting a nursery. The roles concern skilled people someone can meet, rather than offices controlling permission. The new abilities describe learnable actions; the existing spell articles continue to own their supernatural effects and limits.

## Boundaries retained

Lithren's pressure, heat and biological details use conventional physical mechanisms. New finds establish local observations without identifying the ancient enemy, the causes of ecological change or the origin of resonance. Modern names classify observed specimens and constructions rather than fixing the range of possible ruins.

The excursion articles retain their particular unresolved facts. Issava's ordinary responses do not explain all of Olenvar's choices. Ilath does not establish future sight. Savrels provide signals without supplying a common language. Vell giving can travel as a custom without making the guest phenomenon portable. Enuli companions interpret experiences without identifying the inhabitants below Selk.

No private GM explanation, human-review declaration or game-engine-specific procedure was added. The editorial authoring method now considers reusable subjects alongside named ones and applies graph completion explicitly to Atlas work. Its qualitative questions are prompts for judgment, not mandatory fields or per-location quotas.

## Research

External references supplied limited details; all setting-specific subjects and consequences are authored fiction.

- [Monterey Bay Aquarium: bloody-belly comb jelly](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/bloodybelly-comb-jelly) informed irul's reflected colors and the concealment of luminous prey. The animal's social use, habitat connections and keeping problems were developed for Korvath.
- [NASA: Juno views of Europa's icy shell](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/juno/nasas-juno-provides-high-definition-views-of-europas-icy-shell/) supplied attention to surface deposits and possible subsurface liquid. Nelvar is a locally observable pressure release, not an explanation of Lithren's whole climate history.
- [Magic: Planeswalker's Guide to Ikoria](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-ikoria-2020-04-02) prompted attention to distinct ways of living beside dangerous animals. The new keepers, nurseries and collections use their own biological and social relationships.
- [Star Trek: Our Eyes Opened](https://www.startrek.com/news/our-eyes-opened), discussing *Darmok*, informed the difference between repeatable signs and shared meaning. Savrels improve what people can compare while leaving their correspondents and interpretations open.
