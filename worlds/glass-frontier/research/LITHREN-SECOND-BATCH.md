# Lithren: Anaret and the inner works

This batch adds fifteen entries and expands nine existing entries. It completes M4's development of the district behind Ithara Gate. The earlier twenty-entry batch remains the foundation for life in Ithara and journeys to Damarat and Oravel.

## The place worth reaching

**[Anaret](../world/geographic_location/anaret.rb)** is a terraced workshop district around a deep machinery court. Three approaches offer different possibilities: a broad freight descent held by raiders, Ysil's narrow upper cut, and a service cover that could admit either a rescue cradle or an armed party.

Inside are an optical workroom, a service circuit beneath the floor, component racks seen through a camera, workshops across a broken suspended deck and a lower door buried from within. Modern pressure shelters let crews remain in parts of the ruins. The district's buried extent remains open.

A successful gate restoration could reconnect the freight descent and upper workshop thresholds, letting people move equipment and inhabit recovered rooms. It would also restore known old defects. The return remains unperformed; the current exploration and rescue opportunities use existing routes.

## Three useful discoveries

- **[Lethai](../world/artifact/lethai.rb):** an ancient optical array that directs supplied light around curved workpieces. Weak test light reveals its paths; sufficient power lets it join or cut material. Branches extend into neighboring rooms. One cracked mount preserves a dangerous secondary path in the state being considered for restoration.
- **[Sovan](../world/artifact/sovan.rb):** a repaired service carriage whose gripping feet follow tracks under the floor. Its cam repeats a tool movement, and a modern camera reveals parts of a store nobody has yet entered. The old working sequence would carry that camera through a heated branch unless the branch remains shuttered.
- **[Eneth](../world/artifact/eneth.rb):** a broken adjustable workway suspended on translucent structural bands. It offers access above the occupiers, but the temporary crossing has not yet carried a loaded patient cradle. Salvagers below are preparing to remove a band that supports the route.

The **[Lethai Trial](../world/incident/lethai_trial.rb)** gives the first batch a concrete payoff: a previously unidentified Damarat setting makes a replacement part for Sovan. Comparing shapes, manufacturing it and testing the repair establishes one answer about ancient machinery. Other settings and the larger history remain unresolved.

The Encyclopedia adds **vair** optical arrays, **nel** service carriages and **sevril** structural fiber. Each has recognizable signs, uses and variations for further discoveries.

## The current struggle

The **[Araket crew and its captain](../world/faction/araket_crew.rb)** seized the lower approach after hearing about the successful trial. They want portable machinery, valuable cord and a departure with enough profit to justify the risk. They hold cutters for forced recovery work. Their detached ship cabin supplies air and heat to occupiers and captives alike. M5a subsequently consolidated the captain's separate biography into the crew entry.

**[Ysil](../world/npc/ysil.rb)** once traveled with them and helped a captive escape. She wants the upper crossing finished and companions who choose their own journeys. Her former crew contains both friends and people who want her brought back.

The **[orc field physician at the occupied approach](../world/conflict/araket_seizure.rb)** tends injured people on both sides. She wants a route that can actually carry her patients and passage home. Tovin offers extraction; Selven offers armed help and intends his guards to remain afterward. M5a subsequently consolidated her separate biography into the occupation's entry.

The **[Araket Seizure](../world/conflict/araket_seizure.rb)** supports escape, rescue, bargaining, crew defections and direct opposition. Its resolution leaves a district people can continue exploring and using.
